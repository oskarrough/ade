# Changelog

## 0.1.2

- Right-click a thread in the sidebar for Open, Rename, Fork, Copy link and Archive, as a real menu.
- Right-click a message for Quote, Copy and Fork from here; with text selected, Quote and Copy take just that.

## 0.1.1

- The page for a server that's down has a Try now button, which checks at once instead of at the next try.

## 0.1.0

- ade in its own window, with its own name and icon, around any ade server: localhost or a Tailscale address.
- Remembers its size, its place and the thread you closed it on.
- Links to other sites open in your browser.
- A notification when a thread finishes or waits for you while the window is in the background; clicking it opens the thread.
- When the server is down, a page that keeps trying and comes back by itself, with a field to use another server.
- Updates itself.
